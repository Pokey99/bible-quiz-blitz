1 Samuel
Chapter 22

22:1
Q. To what cave did David escape?
A. the cave of Adullam
Q. Who escaped to the cave of Adullam?
A. David
Q. Who went down to David when they heard of his escape?
A. his brothers and all his father’s house

22:2
Q. What three groups gathered to David?
A. everyone who was in distress, and everyone who was in debt, and everyone who was bitter in soul
Q. Who became commander over the people who gathered to him?
A. David
Q. About how many men were with David?
A. about four hundred men
FQ. What can bitter in soul also mean?
A. discontented
FQ. What can discontented also mean?
A. bitter in soul

22:3
SitQ. Who to whom, “Please let my father and my mother stay with you, till I know what God will do for me.”
A. David to the king of Moab.
Q. Where did David go after leaving the cave of Adullam?
A. Mizpeh of Moab
Q. Which two people did David ask to leave with the king of Moab?
A. my father and my mother
Q. With whom did David ask his father and mother to stay?
A. the king of Moab
Q. Until when did David ask his parents to stay with the king of Moab?
A. till I know what God will do for me

22:4
Q. With whom did David leave his parents?
A. the king of Moab
Q. How long did David's parents stay with the king of Moab?
A. all the time that David was in the stronghold
Q. Where was David while his parents stayed with the king of Moab?
A. in the stronghold

22:5
SitQ. Who to whom, “Do not remain in the stronghold; depart, and go into the land of Judah.”
A. Gad to David.
Q. Who was the prophet who instructed David to leave the stronghold?
A. Gad
Q. In what land was David instructed to go instead of remaining in the stronghold?
A. the land of Judah
Q. Into what forest did David go?
A. the forest of Hereth

22:6
Q. Who had been discovered along with the men who were with him?
A. David
Q. Where was Saul sitting when he heard David had been discovered?
A. at Gibeah
Q. Under what tree was Saul sitting at Gibeah?
A. the tamarisk tree
Q. Where was the tamarisk tree under which Saul sat?
A. on the height
Q. What did Saul have in his hand at Gibeah?
A. his spear
Q. Who were standing about Saul at Gibeah?
A. all his servants

22:7
SitQ. Who to whom, “Hear now, people of Benjamin; will the son of Jesse give every one of you fields and vineyards, will he make you all commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, that all of you have conspired against me?”
A. Saul to his servants who stood about him.
Q. Of which people were the servants Saul addressed as they stood about him?
A. Benjamin
Q. What two kinds of property did Saul question whether the son of Jesse would give his servants?
A. fields and vineyards
Q. What two command positions did Saul question whether the son of Jesse would give his servants?
A. commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds

22:8
SitQ. Who to whom, “No one discloses to me when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse.”
A. Saul to his servants who stood about him.
SitQ. Who to whom, “None of you is sorry for me or discloses to me that my son has stirred up my servant against me, to lie in wait, as at this day.”
A. Saul to his servants who stood about him.
Q. With whom had Saul's son made a covenant, according to Saul's accusation?
A. the son of Jesse
Q. What did Saul accuse his son of stirring up his servant to do?
A. to lie in wait

22:9
SitQ. Who to whom, “I saw the son of Jesse coming to Nob, to Ahimelech the son of Ahitub, and he inquired of the Lord for him and gave him provisions and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine.”
A. Doeg the Edomite to Saul.
Q. Who stood by the servants of Saul and reported seeing David at Nob?
A. Doeg the Edomite
Q. Where had Doeg seen the son of Jesse come?
A. Nob
Q. To whom had the son of Jesse come at Nob?
A. Ahimelech the son of Ahitub
Q. Whose son was Ahimelech?
A. Ahitub's

22:10
Q. Of whom had Ahimelech inquired for David, according to Doeg's report?
A. the Lord
Q. What had Ahimelech given David besides a sword, according to Doeg's report?
A. provisions
Q. Whose sword had Ahimelech given David, according to Doeg's report?
A. Goliath the Philistine's

22:11
Q. Which priest did the king summon with all his father's house?
A. Ahimelech the priest, the son of Ahitub
Q. Where were the priests whom Saul summoned?
A. Nob
Q. Who came to the king when summoned with Ahimelech?
A. all his father’s house, the priests who were at Nob

22:12
SitQ. Who to whom, “Hear now, son of Ahitub.”
A. Saul to Ahimelech.
SitQ. Who to whom, “Here I am, my lord.”
A. Ahimelech to Saul.

22:13
SitQ. Who to whom, “Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of Jesse, in that you have given him bread and a sword and have inquired of God for him, so that he has risen against me, to lie in wait, as at this day?”
A. Saul to Ahimelech.
Q. With whom did Saul accuse Ahimelech of conspiring against him?
A. the son of Jesse
Q. What two things did Saul accuse Ahimelech of giving David?
A. bread and a sword
Q. Of whom did Saul accuse Ahimelech of inquiring for David?
A. God

22:14
SitQ. Who to whom, “And who among all your servants is so faithful as David, who is the king’s son-in-law, and captain over your bodyguard, and honored in your house?”
A. Ahimelech to Saul.
Q. Who was described by Ahimelech as faithful among Saul's servants?
A. David
Q. What family relationship did David have to the king?
A. the king’s son-in-law
Q. Over whom was David captain, according to Ahimelech's defense?
A. your bodyguard
Q. Where was David honored, according to Ahimelech's defense?
A. in your house

22:15
SitQ. Who to whom, “Is today the first time that I have inquired of God for him?”
A. Ahimelech to Saul.
SitQ. Who to whom, “No!”
A. Ahimelech to Saul.
SitQ. Who to whom, “Let not the king impute anything to his servant or to all the house of my father, for your servant has known nothing of all this, much or little.”
A. Ahimelech to Saul.
Q. To what group besides himself did Ahimelech ask the king not to impute anything?
A. all the house of my father
Q. How much did Ahimelech claim to have known of the alleged conspiracy?
A. nothing of all this, much or little

22:16
SitQ. Who to whom, “You shall surely die, Ahimelech, you and all your father’s house.”
A. Saul to Ahimelech.
Q. Who was condemned to die along with Ahimelech?
A. all your father’s house

22:17
SitQ. Who to whom, “Turn and kill the priests of the Lord, because their hand also is with David, and they knew that he fled and did not disclose it to me.”
A. Saul to the guard who stood about him.
Q. Whom did Saul command his guard to kill?
A. the priests of the Lord
Q. Why did Saul order the priests killed?
A. because their hand also is with David, and they knew that he fled and did not disclose it to me
Q. Who would not put out their hand to strike the priests of the Lord?
A. the servants of the king

22:18
SitQ. Who to whom, “You turn and strike the priests.”
A. Saul to Doeg.
Q. Who turned and struck down the priests?
A. Doeg the Edomite
Q. How many persons did Doeg kill that day?
A. eighty-five persons
Q. What did the persons Doeg killed wear?
A. the linen ephod

22:19
Q. What city of the priests did Doeg put to the sword?
A. Nob
Q. What was Nob called in connection with the priests?
A. the city of the priests
Q. What four groups of people in Nob were put to the sword?
A. man and woman, child and infant
Q. What three kinds of livestock in Nob were put to the sword?
A. ox, donkey and sheep

22:20
Q. Which son of Ahimelech escaped and fled after David?
A. Abiathar
Q. Who was Abiathar's father?
A. Ahimelech
Q. Who was the father of Ahimelech, whose son Abiathar escaped?
A. Ahitub
Q. After whom did Abiathar flee?
A. David

22:21
Q. Whom had Saul killed, according to Abiathar's report to David?
A. the priests of the Lord

22:22
SitQ. Who to whom, “I knew on that day, when Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would surely tell Saul.”
A. David to Abiathar.
SitQ. Who to whom, “I have occasioned the death of all the persons of your father’s house.”
A. David to Abiathar.
Q. Who did David know would surely tell Saul?
A. Doeg the Edomite
Q. Who accepted responsibility for occasioning the death of Abiathar's father's house?
A. David

SitQ. Who to whom, “Stay with me; do not be afraid, for he who seeks my life seeks your life.”
A. David to Abiathar.
SitQ. Who to whom, “With me you shall be in safekeeping.”
A. David to Abiathar.
Q. With whom was Abiathar to stay in safekeeping?
A. David

A. Abiathar's
